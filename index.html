Published numbers, not commentary — these are what the research notes are written about, available sooner. Sell-side research itself is client-licensed and cannot be pulled; the consensus panel on Positions is where those banks' views actually surface.

Correlation

Brighter means the two move together. Diversification only works where this is dark. Pairs are compared only on days both markets were open, so a Dutch public holiday cannot masquerade as a day Microsoft did not move.

Efficient frontier

A diagnostic, not an instruction. Mean-variance optimisation is very sensitive to its inputs — nudge one expected-return estimate by a percentage point and the "optimal" weights can swing wildly. Useful for noticing unintended concentration. Not a portfolio to copy.
